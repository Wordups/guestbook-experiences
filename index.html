The Booth

Pick up. You're live.

A full-length mirror, a vintage phone and a camera that gets the whole look. Guests lift the receiver, the mirror counts them in, and they leave you a message.

Every outfit. Every pose.
Every word.

Head to toe.

The camera sits above the mirror and frames the whole person, so the outfit makes the video too.

Nothing to figure out.

Lifting the receiver starts everything. Hanging up saves it. There are no buttons to explain.

Words in the mirror.

A screen behind two-way glass puts the countdown right in their reflection.

Make it yours.

Names, date, welcome line, countdown, thank-you screen, colors and logos. Type yours in, then pick up the phone.
